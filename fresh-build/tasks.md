## 24 October real-money launch decision

The owner requested a public launch accepting real donations by 24 October. **Current decision: NO-GO for real money.** The local cash browser journey below is a synthetic test; it does not authorize a live account or public publication. Complete these gates in order and retain dated evidence before changing the decision:

1. **Masjid authority and policy:** the full Board of Trustees acting together approves donor consent/opt-out, public receipt page and PDF fields, salary visibility and retention in a recorded decision. Test the approved fields and access against database grants and rendered web/PDF output.
2. **Real setup and money:** two authorized operators independently verify official identity/contact, real bank/UPI payee and QR, opening balances and site coordinate against source records, then sign the reconciliation. Test a low-value real transaction through receipt, ledger and account statement with a documented reversal procedure.
3. **Production isolation and recovery:** named owners provision separate staging and production, HTTPS, secrets and signing key custody. Apply every migration to a clean staging candidate, run rollback-only fixtures there, capture encrypted database plus private-object backups offsite, restore to a clean authorized target and compare hashes/counts with measured recovery time.
