# President profile payment QR control

**Date:** 24 October 2026 (India time). The owner specified that the President can update the QR from his profile and the replacement becomes the QR in use.

The web profile now offers the President an active bank/UPI account selector, private current-QR view, replacement image preview, payee confirmation and activation. It uses the existing authenticated `/api/management/payment-qr` upload/read routes. Migration `20261024024056_president_only_payment_qr.sql` and the server route now allow only the President to check QR upload eligibility and activate a replacement; Data Entry is denied at both boundaries. Bounded image validation, private Storage verification, immutable version history and the one-active-version constraint remain in force.

The database retires the old active version when a new one is activated. **New** monthly requests capture the new version. Requests already created retain their saved account, QR version and digest so a later change cannot silently redirect an earlier payment instruction. This is the intended meaning of “updated one will be the QR” for future requests.
