# Phone end-to-end test on Android emulators, 17–18 November 2026

## Setup

- Four Android 16 emulators (Pixel 7 profile, Chrome 133) on the owner's Mac, driven through Chrome's DevTools connection so every step is a real tap or typed value in the real page. The Mac has 16 GB of memory, so four phones ran at once and the eight roles took turns on them.
- The web app ran locally (`next start`, production build) against a fresh local Supabase stack with all 39 migrations. Phones reached it through `adb reverse`, so no test account, password or record touched staging or production.
- The first System Admin was made with `backend/scripts/bootstrap_system_admin.py --local`. Every other account was created in the app.
- Phone GPS came from the emulator (`adb emu geo fix`); where the emulator's location provider stalled, Chrome's location was set through DevTools. The server's 100 m check ran unchanged either way.

## Problems found and fixed

1. After sign-in everyone landed on "Monthly contributors" with a cramped line of small links. **Fixed:** a new Home screen with large buttons for the screens each role can use; sign-in opens it and every management page links "← Home".
2. Raw codes were shown instead of plain messages on 14 pages (for example `outside_masjid_area`, `ENROLLMENT_DISABLED`, `not_authorized`). **Fixed:** every page error now goes through the shared plain-language converter, and about 60 common database codes have messages; any other input check says "Check the details and try again."
3. A deactivated person saw "Checking your account…" forever next to `not_authorized`; meetings and finance pages hung the same way on any load failure. **Fixed:** the page stops checking and shows "This account cannot do that, or it has been deactivated. Ask the President."
4. The task review list showed account IDs instead of usernames, and statuses as codes (`completion_submitted`). **Fixed:** usernames and plain statuses ("Completion sent for review", "Completed and accepted").
5. WhatsApp numbers had to be typed as `+919876543210`; a 10-digit number was refused with "Please match the requested format". **Fixed:** 10-digit Indian numbers (with spaces, a leading 0 or 91) are accepted and stored as +91.
6. After a successful contributor enrollment or payment reference, the form reset used a reference React had already cleared, so the page would have shown an error although the save worked. **Fixed** on both forms.
7. Dates were empty on donation, direct cash, expense payment, advance payment and salary forms, and the salary amount was empty. **Fixed:** today's India date and the monthly salary are filled in and can be changed.
8. Recording returned advance money asked for a "Bill or receipt reference". **Fixed:** the field is "Bill, receipt or return slip reference".
9. Check-in gave no sign it was waiting for GPS (up to 20 seconds) and the button stayed tappable. **Fixed:** the button reads "Finding your location…" and is disabled while waiting, for check-in and for organizer marking. A failed organizer mark no longer hides the roster.
10. The Treasurer's opening balance form appears only after choosing an account, with no hint. **Fixed:** a hint under the account picker.
11. Creating a meeting or task and inviting someone gave no confirmation. **Fixed:** "Meeting created…", "Task created…", "Invitation sent." and messages for join, leave, completion and review.
12. Announcements could not be published until a date and time were picked. **Fixed:** an empty publish time means "Publish now".
13. Enrolling a contributor from a phone failed with "Check the contributor details" whenever the phone's clock ran even one second ahead of the server, because the consent time arrived in the future. **Fixed:** the server never accepts a future consent time.
14. `bootstrap_system_admin.py` could not run on the local stack (one statement per query there). **Fixed:** it now runs as one all-or-nothing statement, takes `--local`, and reports the database's reason without the password.

## Volunteer WhatsApp messages (owner decision 17 November)

Until the WhatsApp Business API is integrated, volunteers send from their own WhatsApp.

- Migration `20261118120000_volunteer_whatsapp_send.sql`: `list_messageable_donors` lists the donors a person may message for a month (without numbers); `prepare_donor_whatsapp` hands out one donor's number for one message. Only the donor's referrer, the Treasurer and the President get numbers, only for active donors who have not opted out; the reminder is refused once the month is verified as paid; System Admin gets none. Every hand-out is recorded in an append-only table.
- The contributor list shows "Send monthly request on WhatsApp" and "Send reminder on WhatsApp" (or "Paid for this month") to those people only. The button opens the volunteer's WhatsApp with the donor chosen and the message typed; the volunteer taps Send.
- Receipt codes are not sent this way; that stays behind the receipt handoff approval.
- Tested on the emulator: a member enrolled two donors they referred (typing `98765 00011` and `09876500022`, stored as +91); their buttons opened WhatsApp's chat page for +91 98765 00011 with the November 2026 message and ₹300; a Committee Member who did not refer them saw no buttons; the send was recorded. The emulator has no WhatsApp app, so the web chat page opened instead of the app. The test file `volunteer_whatsapp.sql` proves the referrer, other member, System Admin, Treasurer, opted-out, wrong-kind and mid-month rules and the record. The paid-month reminder refusal is in the function but not yet in a test.
- Local only: enrollment was switched on with a test consent version to create donors. Staging and production keep enrollment off until the Board approves the consent wording.
