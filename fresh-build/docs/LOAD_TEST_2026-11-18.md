# Traffic test, 18 November 2026

## What ran

- **4 Android emulator phones** (Chrome) signed in as members and kept opening Tasks, Meetings, Notifications and Home, timing how long each screen took until its content appeared.
- **Virtual phones** from `backend/scripts/phone_load_test.mjs`: each signs in through the real sign-in and first password change, then repeats what a phone does — open Home, role check, Tasks (and join a task with 40 places), Meetings, Notifications, Activity points, and submit a cash donation every third round — with a 1–3 second pause between taps like a person.
- Against a local copy (production build of the web app, local Supabase with all 41 migrations) on the owner's Mac (16 GB, Apple silicon), shared with the four emulators.
- Ten or more emulators do not fit in 16 GB (about 2 GB each; one of four shut down in the 17 November test), so the extra phones were virtual. To the server they are the same as real phones.

## Correctness under load

- The task had 40 places and 50 phones tried to join it at the same moment: exactly **40 joined and 10 waitlisted**.
- **296 donations** were reported as submitted and exactly 296 are stored: none lost, none doubled.
- 50 accounts did their first sign-in and password change from one address without being blocked (see the fix below).

## Problem found and fixed

First-time password changes were limited to **5 per 10 minutes per internet address**. At the masjid every phone on the same Wi-Fi shares one address, and mobile networks often share addresses too, so the sixth tester to set a password within 10 minutes would have been told "Too many attempts". The limit now counts **per sign-in session** (5 per 10 minutes, the token is stored only as a hash) with a looser cap of **60 per 10 minutes per address** so guessing from one place is still slowed. Sign-in itself was already counted per username.

## Live staging site (public pages only)

10 visitors for 30 seconds on `https://masjid-e-mamoor-2.vercel.app` (home, announcements, sign-in, app manifest, and the signed-out API answer): 450 requests, **0 errors**, typical 46–112 ms. The slowest home page load was 2.3 s, the usual first-request wake-up on Vercel's free plan. No accounts were used on staging.

## What this means and what it does not

- For a masjid committee (tens of people, rarely more than a handful at the same moment), the app and database have a wide margin: 50 people using it at once kept replies under 0.3 s and the stress run reached about 15 times that load.
- The numbers are for this laptop. The hosted staging database runs on Supabase's free plan, which has less processing power and pauses after a week without use; signed-in load on staging was not tested, to keep test accounts off a shared environment. A short signed-in test on staging, or on production before launch, is the remaining check.
- Phone screens on real devices are usually faster than these emulators.
