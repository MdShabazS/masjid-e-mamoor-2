# Phase 1 — primary-source research log

**Checked:** 15 October 2026; India privacy rules and WhatsApp policy rechecked 16 October 2026. **Scope:** engineering choices for Masjid E Mamoor 2; recheck sources, platform accounts and prices during integration and just before launch. This log distinguishes a documented provider fact from our design inference. No provider account, approved message template, legal opinion or production environment was obtained by reading documentation.

| Topic and primary source | Verified guidance | Product decision or next verification |
|---|---|---|
| [Supabase database functions](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/functions) and [RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security) | Function `EXECUTE` grants and owner privileges must be managed explicitly; a security-definer function can bypass caller RLS. Exposed tables need RLS and appropriate grants. | Use narrow RPCs with active actor and object checks in body, explicit revoke/grant, protected schemas. Test actual `anon`, active roles, inactive users and service routes; `search_path` alone does not authorize a caller. This is an engineering inference from the trust model. |
| [Supabase testing overview](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/local-development/testing/overview) | Supabase documents local database testing and pgTAP for schema, RLS and functions. | Require real Postgres role/RPC, concurrency and scenario tests before treating draft SQL as complete. Node tests do not cover database behavior. |
| [Supabase backups](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups) and [restore guidance](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/migrating-within-supabase/dashboard-restore) | Database backup contains object metadata, not Storage object bytes. A database restore alone cannot recreate deleted object contents. | Build separate encrypted database-plus-object archive with manifest/hash and clean staging restore drill. Confirm plan-specific backup and recovery capabilities before launch. |
| [Expo APK guide](https://docs.expo.dev/build-reference/apk/), [Android build](https://docs.expo.dev/build-reference/android-builds/) and [app credentials](https://docs.expo.dev/app-signing/app-credentials/) | Installable Android APK build configuration differs from a JavaScript export; Android installation/update depends on signing. | `expo export --platform android` remains a bundle check only. Build signed release APK, keep package/signing identity, test install and upgrade on a device, and safeguard keystore. |
| [Meta template categories](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-categorization), [templates](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview), [webhooks](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/overview) | Meta classifies templates and delivers inbound/status events by webhook. | Design provider inbox/outbox with authenticated ingress and event dedupe. Submit exact donation/reminder/receipt copy for actual Meta category and approval; do not label fundraising requests `utility` from our own inference. |
| [WhatsApp Business policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/) and [getting opt-in](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in) | The recipient must give their number/username and opt in to subsequent WhatsApp contact; the business must respect stop requests, use approved templates for initiated Platform conversations and maintain a published privacy policy. Rechecked 16 October. | A referring member's assertion alone is insufficient evidence of recipient opt-in. Record the donor's consent wording/version/method/time/evidence separately; suppress STOP/wrong-number before all new/retry sends. Draft notice needs masjid approval. |
| [WhatsApp Business Platform pricing](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/) | Pricing depends on delivered message, category and destination market; published rates can change. | Do not hardcode an India rupee cost into requirements. At onboarding, get approved template categories and current India rate card, estimate first-Friday/reminder volume and set budget/alerts. |
| [Vercel environments](https://vercel.com/docs/deployments/environments) and [limits](https://vercel.com/docs/limits) | Local, Preview and Production environments can carry distinct variables; resource limits depend on platform configuration. | If Vercel is chosen, test preview and production settings, scheduled jobs, file/PDF rendering constraints and costs against selected plan. Hosting choice is still a candidate, not a deployed fact. |
| [India DPDP Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf), [2025 Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) and [commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) | Rules and Act provisions have phased commencement; it would be inaccurate to assume every provision has the same effective date. Rechecked the published Rules 16 October. The seven-year record provision in their Consent Manager schedule is for a registered Consent Manager, not a blanket masjid donor-retention rule. | Obtain professional/local organizational review of applicable duties, notice, consent, retention, access and grievance process before handling real donor/member data. Engineering default is minimal projection, documented purpose, restricted access and no unapproved destructive purge; this is not legal advice or a declaration of compliance. |
