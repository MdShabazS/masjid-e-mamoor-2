Masjid E Mamoor 2 · test version

Phone test guide

How to install the app on your phone, sign in, and what to try for your role. Everything here is test data. No real money moves.

The app's address

https://masjid-e-mamoor-2.vercel.app

Test only. Use made-up amounts and names. Never enter real bank details, real donor phone numbers or your real passwords from other apps.

Passwords are shared in person or by phone call, never in a WhatsApp group.

1. Add it to your home screen

iPhone

  1. Open the address in Safari (or Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Mamoor 2 from your home screen.

Android

  1. Open the address in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Open Mamoor 2 from your home screen.

2. Sign in for the first time

  1. Tap Management sign in.
  2. Enter the username and temporary password you were given (four groups of four letters and numbers, like ABCD-2345-EFGH-6789).
  3. The app asks you to choose your own password: at least 12 characters. Write it somewhere safe.
  4. You land on Home: large buttons for the screens your role can use, grouped as Money, Donors, Work and Messages. Each screen has a "← Home" link at the top.

3. Setup before testing (organizer)

Do these once, in this order, before the testers start.

  1. System Admin (sys.admin) → People and roles → add the President. Give the shown password to them privately.
  2. System Admin → Meetings → Masjid location for check-in: enter the latitude and longitude of the place where you will test (in Google Maps, long-press the spot to see them). For this test it is the test place, not the real masjid.
  3. President → People and roles → add the Treasurer, Vice President and Member.
  4. President → Finance → Accounts → Create account: a Cash account, for example "Test cash box".
  5. Treasurer → Finance → Accounts → Submit opening balance: ₹10,000.
  6. President → Finance → Accounts → Verify pending openings. The cash box now shows ₹10,000.
  7. Vice President → Meetings → schedule a meeting for today at the test place and invite the Member and the Treasurer.

When something looks wrong

Send the organizer a message with:

  • your phone (for example "iPhone 13" or "Redmi Note 12") and your role
  • what you tapped, in order
  • what you expected, and what happened instead
  • a screenshot of the screen
