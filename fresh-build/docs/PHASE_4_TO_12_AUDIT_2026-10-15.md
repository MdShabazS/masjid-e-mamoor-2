## Cross-phase misses and contradictions found

1. **P0 — no executable database proof.** All 13 fresh migrations and 8 SQL fixture files remain unrun on real Postgres. A passing Node suite or web/Metro compilation cannot establish SQL syntax, constraints, `SECURITY DEFINER`/RLS grants, concurrent balances, scheduled jobs or report/receipt results. Provision an isolated fresh development project or local Postgres before calling any of Phases 4–12 complete.
2. **P0 — identity path differs between source layers.** `backend/identity/service.js` defines username login, active gate and mandatory first password change, but `apps/web/app/login/page.jsx` and `apps/android/App.jsx` still call Supabase email sign-in directly. The proposed `/v1` handler has no mounted production route or durable limiter. The Phase 5 web/Android enrollment forms invoke `enroll_monthly_donor`, whose authenticated EXECUTE grant migration 2 revokes. The newer `enroll_versioned_monthly_donor` is service-only and requires an approved consent version (currently absent). The displayed enrollment form is therefore not a working fresh-backend journey. Do not bypass the gate to make the form appear functional.
3. **P0 — correction and document chain is missing.** Phase 4's Treasurer correction/void and Phase 5/6 contribution and expense corrections must change active balance, receipt validity, referral/score effects and Phase 7 report revisions together. The Phase 7 schema accepts only `queued` status and has no PDF worker. Treat financial exports and public receipts as unavailable until this chain is tested.
4. **P1 — clients trail backend source.** The fresh web and Android apps mainly expose early contribution flows. Phases 4, 6–9 and much of 10 have database drafts but no complete client navigation, forms, status and error flows. Phase 12's task/expense/donation offline entry panel does not substitute for online management journeys.
5. **P1 — server workers and private object handling trail queues.** First-Friday/reminder, meeting reminder, member inbox and WhatsApp outbox rows are not proof of scheduled delivery. The QR registry needs an authenticated server upload/byte-digest path; private memo, bill, receipt and report assets need byte/type checks, scoped read, retention and staging restore. WhatsApp cannot render an approved QR message yet.
6. **P1 — operational recovery is absent.** The checked source `.tar.gz` contains neither actual Postgres rows nor Storage object bytes. An owner/Drive/key setup, encrypted job and clean staging restore are still needed before product release. Android SecureStore drafts are local-only and require device/upgrade testing.
7. **P1 — owner and field gates remain.** Phase 1 owner decisions affect finance approval, donor grants/consent/privacy, referral cap, public receipts and salaries. Real QR/account, masjid location, Business number/templates, copy and roster, staging users, signing and Drive identity are still needed at their respective activation gates. Independent source development can continue without them.
8. **Documentation drift corrected during this audit.** The Phase 4 checkpoint had listed account pagination and reconciliation observations as unbuilt even though migration 4 contains both. The Phase 5 checkpoint called QR activation P/L while the SQL currently grants President/data-entry. The master plan summary still claimed 18 tests and omitted the newer offline/WhatsApp source; those lines were updated. These corrections do not establish database execution.

## Recommended build order after this review

1. Repair the Phase 0/3 reproducible setup, bring up an isolated fresh DB, apply all migrations and run the role fixtures. Fix observed SQL defects, then add concurrency/direct-access tests where a financial or privileged transition lacks them.
2. Connect username/password-change identity and the consent-versioned enrollment service to both clients. Obtain owner approval before real donor activation.
3. Close finance correction/void and private asset handling; then render receipts/reports and reconcile exact output with the posted ledger.
4. Complete online donation/expense, task, meeting/attendance and content client flows with their server workers and acceptance tests.
5. Keep WhatsApp live sending disabled until the masjid number, approved templates and real QR delivery are configured. Complete operational DB plus object backup/restore and device replay drills before launch.

**Completion answer:** none of Phases 4–12 has met its defined exit criteria. They contain different degrees of local source progress; the work above is not an assertion that every source implementation is defect-free.
