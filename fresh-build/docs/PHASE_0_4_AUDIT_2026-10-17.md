## Disposable local recovery rehearsal

A full `pg_dump -Fc` from the fresh local PostgreSQL database could not be
restored by the local `postgres` role into scratch: a Supabase-managed Realtime
function attempted to set `log_min_messages`. Excluding Realtime exposed a
Vault `secrets` table permission error. The targeted archive therefore covered
`public`, `app_private`, `auth`, `storage` and `extensions` only. It restored
with `pg_restore --no-owner --no-acl --exit-on-error` into a fresh scratch
database after removing that database's default empty `public` schema.

The targeted archive SHA-256 was
`c311c4a17214201968b391c907d474b223eaaa522b15c19485f484ccd2c8196a`.
Source and scratch both had two Auth users, two app users, one account, one
beneficiary, one verified opening, one finance effect, and a 12,345-paise
effect sum. Separately, a 68-byte synthetic object in `qr-private` was backed
up, deleted, replayed and byte-checked at SHA-256
`a5d47cdc553346c0f101040852c3b79700b04675468724e9846e985875ae99d3`.
This proves local targeted row and object-byte recovery, not an integrated
hosted backup system or a complete managed-schema restore. The scratch
database/dumps were removed, the fresh local database was reset through all
16 migrations at that recovery drill, and final counts were zero users, effects, private objects and
scratch databases.
